MORNING CAMP — 09:00–12:00

5 days · Monday–Friday · 15 hours of structured professional coaching

Start your day with focused padel training and use five consecutive mornings to make a real difference to your game.

The Morning Camp is designed for players who want a structured and intensive training experience without taking up their entire day. You will train in a small group with professional coaches, with a maximum of 4 players per court, allowing the coaches to give you individual attention, technical corrections and tactical guidance throughout the sessions.

Across the week, training combines technical development with real-game situations. You will work on your defensive game, transition to the net, attacking patterns, overheads, positioning, partner coordination and tactical decision-making.

The key difference is that you don't simply repeat shots. You learn when to use them, why to use them and how to build a point around them.

With 15 hours of quality coaching over five days, the Morning Camp gives you enough continuity to understand your game, work on specific weaknesses and turn new techniques into better decisions on court.

Perfect for: players who want maximum training focus while keeping their afternoons and evenings free.

EVENING CAMP — 17:30–20:30

5 days · Monday–Friday · 15 hours of structured professional coaching

Want to train seriously without changing your daytime schedule? The Evening Camp gives you five consecutive days of focused padel training with professional coaches — while keeping your mornings and afternoons free.

From Monday to Friday, you will train for 3 hours per day, giving you a total of 15 hours of quality coaching throughout the camp.

Training takes place in small groups with a maximum of 4 players per court, allowing coaches to work closely with every player and provide individual technical corrections, tactical guidance and practical feedback.

The program combines technical development with real-game situations. You will work on positioning, defense, transition, net play, attacking patterns, overheads and tactical decision-making. Instead of simply hitting more balls, you will learn when to use each shot, why to use it and how to build the point together with your partner.

The evening format is ideal if you work, have daytime commitments or simply prefer training later in the day. You get the full The Padel Camp experience without having to dedicate your daytime hours to the camp.

Perfect for: players who want serious padel development without changing their daytime schedule.

OUR APPROACH

What makes our training different

  • Personalized Development — Every player receives individual attention. We assess your current level and create a tailored improvement plan that targets your specific weaknesses while building on your strengths.
  • Learn from the Best — Train under professional coaches from Spain, the global epicenter of padel. Absorb techniques and strategies used at the highest competitive levels.
  • Real Match Experience — Theory means nothing without practice. Daily competitive matches let you apply new skills under pressure, building confidence and match intelligence.
  • Complete Player Growth — We develop all aspects of your game: technical skills, tactical awareness, physical conditioning, and mental resilience. You leave as a more complete player.
  • Attack-First Philosophy — Modern padel rewards proactive play. We teach you to control the net, dictate rallies, and put opponents under constant pressure with aggressive positioning.
  • Precision & Mobility — Master the art of court coverage. Quick feet, smart positioning, and precise shot placement are the foundation of winning padel.
  • Champion Mindset — Technical skill alone doesn't win matches. We train mental toughness: staying calm under pressure, recovering from mistakes, and maintaining focus throughout long rallies.
  • Intensive Immersion — Multiple daily sessions combine drills, point play, video analysis, and theory. This immersive approach accelerates learning far beyond weekly lessons.
